# SmartSeat Application

Smartseat is a seat reservation and information application for students and lecturers. Smartseat tracks the status of the seat to give users live information on the occupancy, features and conditions of the seats an the rooms. With Smartseat, students and lecturers can reserve their seats and rooms for their classes, lessons and meeting. With AI/ML features, Smartseat recommends the users the most suitable and comfortable seats as well as with forecasting, Smartseat informs the users of how the situation will be in the future.

# Features

- Live data, seatmap and updates on the seats and rooms
- Reserve, confirm and cancel seat/room booking
- AI seat recommendation and forecasting
- Heatmap and Utilization report of the seats/rooms
- Seats/Rooms reservation history
- Admin dashboard on details of users, seats, rooms
